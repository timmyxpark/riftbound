Booster Boxes
The display box each case is built from. A case runs six to eleven boxes depending on the set, so the two tables should track each other. Sealed asks are TCGplayer's own lowest listed price and include shipping, unlike the card sections: the raw listing feed carries mislabelled items under sealed ids - a $180 "Origins Booster Box" sat under the real $244.78 - and TCGplayer's own figure already excludes them. Shipping is 1-5% on a sealed box, where on a five-cent card it was everything.

Other Sealed Products
Bundles and box sets that do not belong to a booster set, so the set chips above do not narrow this table. Asks come from TCGplayer's own lowest listed price and include shipping, as in the two tables above. Their RPT and EVT chips read neutral: Riot publishes no reprint schedule for these and they sit outside the competitive calendar, so those two factors are left unscored rather than guessed, and the signal rests on value against the floor and momentum alone.

What the signals mean
Score is the weighted blend of the four factor chips, each running -2 to +2: VAL value against the floor (40%), MOM momentum (25%), RPT reprint risk (20%), EVT event demand (15%).

Deck Pricer
Build a list and price it three ways at once. Asking is the cheapest Near Mint English copy listed, card price only, no shipping - what you would have to undercut to sell; a card with nothing listed borrows its own sold average instead of counting as zero, and the cell says so. Avg Last 5 Sold is the mean of that card's last five real sales - closer to what it actually moves for. Max(Ask, Sold) takes whichever is higher for each card and totals those.
